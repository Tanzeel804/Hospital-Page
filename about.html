About MediCare+

Leading healthcare provider dedicated to excellence and patient care

Our Story

Founded in 2005, MediCare+ has been a beacon of hope and healing in the healthcare industry. What started as a small clinic has grown into a state-of-the-art hospital with comprehensive medical services.

Our mission is simple: to provide world-class medical care with compassion and professionalism. We believe healthcare is not just about treating diseases, but about caring for people.

Over the years, we've treated thousands of patients and earned their trust through consistent delivery of quality healthcare, innovative treatments, and patient-centric services.
