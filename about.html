Our Story

MediCare Plus started as a small clinic with one simple goal: provide trustworthy and compassionate care. Over the years we expanded into a full hospital providing specialists, emergency services, and modern diagnostics.

We prioritize patient safety, evidence-based medicine, and community wellness programs to make healthcare accessible to all.
