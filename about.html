About MediCare Plus

Leading healthcare provider committed to excellence and patient satisfaction

Our Story

MediCare Plus Hospital was founded in 2010 with a vision to provide world-class healthcare services to the community. Starting with a small clinic, we have grown into one of the most trusted medical institutions in the region.

Our journey has been marked by continuous innovation, investment in cutting-edge technology, and recruitment of the finest medical talent. We believe in combining traditional compassion with modern medicine to deliver the best patient outcomes.

Today, we operate multiple departments, employ over 150 dedicated healthcare professionals, and serve more than 50,000 patients annually.

Our Mission

To deliver exceptional healthcare services with integrity, compassion, and excellence, ensuring every patient receives personalized and evidence-based treatment.

Our Vision

To be a leading healthcare provider recognized for innovation, quality care, and patient satisfaction, advancing medical excellence through continuous research and development.

Our Values

Patient-centered care, medical excellence, integrity, teamwork, and continuous improvement drive every decision we make and every service we provide.
