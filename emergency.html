What To Do In An Emergency

Follow these steps to ensure quick and effective response

Step 1: Stay Calm

Keep calm and assess the situation. Move to a safe location if possible.

Step 2: Call Emergency

Call 911 immediately and describe symptoms clearly. Provide exact location.

Step 3: First Aid

Follow dispatcher's instructions. Perform CPR or first aid if trained.

Step 4: Await Help

Stay on the line with dispatcher. Keep patient immobilized if possible.

In Case of Emergency

Call 911 or contact our ambulance service immediately
